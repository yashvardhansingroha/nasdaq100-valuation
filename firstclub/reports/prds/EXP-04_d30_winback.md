# PRD: EXP-04 — D30 Win-Back for Churned First-Timers

**Status:** Ready for A/B | **ICE:** 7.0 | **Effort:** 3 weeks

## Problem
D30 repeat rate is 28% vs 32% peer median. One-and-done users are 72% of first-order cohort.

## Solution
Day-30 push to users with exactly 1 order: "Staples bundle at ₹299 (worth ₹450) — free delivery."

## Success Metrics
- **Primary:** D30 repeat rate (28% → 34%)
- **Guardrail:** Contribution margin per order (>₹40)

## Scope
- Cohort segmentation (1 order, no D30 return)
- Bundle SKU curation (atta, oil, rice)
- Push + in-app banner

## Out of Scope
- Subscription conversion (see EXP-05)
- Paid re-acquisition campaigns
