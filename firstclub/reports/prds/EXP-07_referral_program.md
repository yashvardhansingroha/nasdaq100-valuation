# PRD: EXP-07 — Referral: Give ₹100 / Get ₹100

**Status:** Ready for A/B | **ICE:** 7.0 | **Effort:** 4 weeks

## Problem
Blended CAC is ₹185 with 35% paid social. Referral channel is under 18% of installs.

## Solution
Double-sided referral: referrer gets ₹100 wallet credit when referee completes first order; referee gets ₹100 off first order (min ₹299).

## Success Metrics
- **Primary:** Blended CAC (−18%)
- **Guardrail:** Referral fraud rate (<2%)

## Scope
- Referral link generation + tracking
- Wallet credit system
- Fraud: device fingerprint, max 5 referrals/month

## Out of Scope
- Tiered referral rewards
- B2B/corporate referral
