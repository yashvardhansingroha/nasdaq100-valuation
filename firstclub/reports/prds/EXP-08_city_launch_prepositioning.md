# PRD: EXP-08 — City Launch: Hyperlocal Dark-Store Pre-Positioning

**Status:** Discovery | **ICE:** 6.3 | **Effort:** 8 weeks

## Problem
New city launches average 22% install-to-first-order in week 1 vs 29% mature cities. Stockouts drive early churn.

## Solution
Pre-stock top 200 SKUs by micro-cluster demand model before any marketing spend. Gate launch on 95% in-stock on top 50 SKUs.

## Success Metrics
- **Primary:** Launch-week first-order rate (+20% vs historical)
- **Guardrail:** Stockout rate (<8% on top 50 SKUs)

## Scope
- Demand model per pincode cluster
- Assortment matrix + safety stock rules
- Pre-launch inventory QA checklist

## Out of Scope
- Franchise/partner store model
- Same-day store build (min 6-week lead)
