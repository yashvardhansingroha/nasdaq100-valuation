# PRD: EXP-05 — Staples Subscription (Weekly Auto-Reorder)

**Status:** Discovery | **ICE:** 6.3 | **Effort:** 6 weeks

## Problem
Orders per active month is 2.4. Staples drive 28% of basket but require manual re-purchase.

## Solution
Subscribe & save: weekly/bi-weekly auto-delivery on top 20 SKUs with 5% discount.

## Success Metrics
- **Primary:** Orders per active month (2.4 → 3.1)
- **Guardrail:** Inventory waste rate (<3%)

## Scope
- Subscription management UI
- Recurring slot in dark-store pick path
- Payment tokenization for auto-charge

## Out of Scope
- Custom subscription boxes
- Pause/skip beyond 2 weeks
