# FirstClub City Launch Playbook

**Use case:** Expand quick-commerce to a new city (template: Pune launch, EXP-08)

---

## T-8 Weeks: Feasibility & Site Selection

- [ ] Pincode-level demand model (search volume, competitor density, avg basket)
- [ ] Identify 3–5 micro-clusters with >15K households within 2 km of candidate dark stores
- [ ] Sign dark store leases (target: 2 stores for launch, 4 by Day 90)
- [ ] Hire City GM + ops lead (local language capability required)

**Gate:** Demand model shows ≥8K addressable weekly orders at 25% penetration

---

## T-6 Weeks: Supply & Catalog

- [ ] Stock top 200 SKUs per dark store (see assortment matrix below)
- [ ] Localize catalog: regional brands, festival SKUs
- [ ] Onboard 2+ local dairy/bread suppliers for freshness edge
- [ ] QA: 95% in-stock on top 50 SKUs during soft launch

### Assortment Matrix (Top Categories)

| Category | SKU Count | In-Stock Target |
|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| Staples (rice, atta, oil) | 40 | 98% |
| Dairy & bread | 25 | 95% |
| Snacks & beverages | 35 | 92% |
| Produce | 30 | 88% |
| Personal care | 35 | 90% |
| Household | 35 | 90% |

---

## T-4 Weeks: Ops & Rider Fleet

- [ ] Recruit 40 riders per store (1.5× peak capacity buffer)
- [ ] Rider training: app flow, hygiene, customer interaction
- [ ] Define delivery SLA: 15 min median, 25 min P95
- [ ] Set up local customer support (chat + phone, 8am–11pm)

---

## T-2 Weeks: Soft Launch

- [ ] Invite-only beta: 500 households per micro-cluster
- [ ] Instrument all event taxonomy events for new `city` property
- [ ] Daily standup: stockout rate, delivery SLA, NPS
- [ ] Target: ≥30% install-to-first-order in beta cohort

**Kill criteria:** Stockout rate >12% for 3 consecutive days → pause marketing

---

## Launch Week (T-0)

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| D-1 | Press + influencer seeding (local food/lifestyle) |
| D0 | Paid social burst (₹8L/day, geo-fenced to serviceable pincodes) |
| D0–D3 | ₹100 off first order (cap 5K redemptions/day) |
| D0–D7 | Referral give/get ₹100 (EXP-07) |
| D7 | First weekly review: FOR, CAC, delivery SLA, review sentiment |

**Success metrics (launch week):**

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Install → first order rate | ≥25% |
| Median delivery time | ≤15 min |
| App store rating (new city reviews) | ≥4.0 |
| Stockout rate (top 50 SKUs) | ≤8% |
| Blended CAC | ≤₹200 |

---

## Day 30 Post-Launch

- [ ] Expand to 2 additional micro-clusters if FOR ≥28% and SLA met
- [ ] Cut paid spend 30% if LTV/CAC < 1.2× (see CAC/LTV simulator)
- [ ] Publish city P&L: revenue, CM1, delivery cost per order
- [ ] Retrospective: update playbook with Pune-specific learnings
